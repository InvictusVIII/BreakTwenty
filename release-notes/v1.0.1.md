# BreakTwenty 1.0.1

## Changes

- Improved RBC historical credit-card syncing when the bank intermittently rejects overlapping requests.
- Made Autosync and Sync All recover automatically from brief internet interruptions and report recovered providers correctly.
- Added a 12-hour or 24-hour clock preference to first-run setup.
- RBC secure login now captures Windows credentials reliably from the bank browser, reopens immediately after a failed attempt, and gives transient credit-card history failures fair delayed recovery without discarding completed windows.
- Transaction fetching status is visually distinct from retry warnings again, with its icon, label, and range progress cleanly centered in the Accounts detail tray.
- RBC secure login now prioritizes the short-lived raw password field during browser capture, closing a remaining Windows timing race without changing how users sign in.
- Standard provider diagnostic exports now redact additional nested account references and opaque RBC routing data from replay samples.
