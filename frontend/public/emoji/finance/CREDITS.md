# Finance Icons — Sources & Licenses

Curated icon pack bundled under `frontend/public/emoji/finance/` and used
by the in-app emoji picker when the Finance set is active.

Every source is permissively licensed and free for commercial use. Full
upstream license texts are reproduced in
[`../THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`](../THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md). The sets used are
MIT (Icons8 `flat-color-icons`, elected from its MIT/Good Boy dual license, and
Microsoft `fluent-color`), CC0-1.0 (spothq `cryptocurrency-color`, public
domain), and Apache-2.0 (Material Design Icons).
